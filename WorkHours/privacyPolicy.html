Privacy Policy – Work Hours & Shift Log

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Work Hours & Shift Log is provided as a mobile app by Programmer's Playground. We are committed to protecting your privacy. We do not directly collect personal information that identifies you, such as your name, phone number, email address, or home address.

Work Hours and Shift Data

Information that you enter in the app, such as work hours, shift times, breaks, notes, and related records, is stored locally on your device. We do not receive or maintain a separate copy of this information on our servers. Removing the app or clearing its data may permanently delete locally stored records unless you have created a backup using a feature available on your device.

Advertisements

We may use third-party advertising networks, including Google AdMob and AppLovin, to display ads within the app. These services may collect and process certain data to show personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on your consent and location.

Advertising data may include device identifiers, approximate location, interactions with ads, and general app usage data. These third parties process such data under their own privacy policies.

Analytics and Diagnostics

We may use Firebase Analytics and related Firebase services to understand how users interact with the app and to improve its stability. This may include general usage information such as screen views, session duration, device type, app version, crash reports, and performance data.

We use this information to improve app performance, reliability, and the user experience. We do not use analytics or diagnostics data to identify you directly.

Data Collection

The app does not require you to create an account, and we do not directly request or store personal details such as your name, phone number, or email address. Third-party services integrated into the app may collect and process data as described in this policy and in their respective privacy policies.

Your Choices and Consent

Depending on your location, including the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app may ask for your consent before using data for personalized advertising or analytics. Where available, you may change your consent choices through the app's privacy or settings options. You can also limit ad personalization through your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Work-hour and shift records remain on your device until you edit or delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Data processed by third-party services is retained according to those providers' respective retention policies.
