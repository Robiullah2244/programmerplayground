Privacy Policy – Dutch–English Translator

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

The Dutch–English Translator app, which supports translations from Dutch to English and from English to Dutch, is free to use. We are committed to protecting your privacy. We do not directly collect personal information that identifies you, such as your name, phone number, or email address.

Translation Content

Text that you enter for translation may be processed by a third-party translation service to provide the requested translation. Please do not enter sensitive or confidential personal information into the app. Translation providers process submitted text according to their own privacy policies.

Advertisements

We use third-party advertising networks, including Google AdMob and AppLovin, to display ads within the app. These services may collect and process certain data to show personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on your consent and location.

Advertising data may include device identifiers, approximate location, interactions with ads, and general app usage data. These third parties process such data under their own privacy policies.

Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the app. This may include usage information such as screen views, session duration, device information, and crash or performance data.

We use this information to improve app performance, reliability, and the user experience. We do not use analytics data to identify you directly.

Data Collection

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not directly request or store personal details such as your name, phone number, or email address. Third-party services integrated into the app may collect and process data as described in this policy and in their respective privacy policies.

Your Choices and Consent

Depending on your location, including the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app may ask for your consent before using data for personalized advertising or analytics. Where available, you may change your consent choices through the app's privacy or settings options. You can also limit ad personalization through your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.
